LL.B Study Books

Interactive study books for the Vikrama Simhapuri University 3-year LL.B syllabus (2025-26 onwards), one for each paper, with practice papers and model answers, plus companion guides on legal maxims, legal writing and bail practice. Law stated as of November 2026.

Each book works offline in any browser: units with case boxes, a searchable case index, self-test quizzes and a revision tracker that saves progress on your device.
